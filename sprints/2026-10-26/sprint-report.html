What you need to test live (Under Review)

  1. The example request with a real model and a real report: "Add the characters from the dungeon to my friends, except the shaman." Check that Barnaby picks or asks about the run, leaves out the shaman and your own characters, and shows a card; that Approve adds them; and that the profile page updates. If you get an error instead of a card, that's sc-241.
  2. Real Warcraft Logs data: "who was in <report>". The playerDetails fields (server, type, specs) are inferred, not recorded (D12). Check that realms and regions come back filled.
  3. Ambiguous run: with no run in the chat, Barnaby should ask, listing up to 5 candidates. He picks the newest only when you say "last" or "latest" (D5).
  4. Role and removal edits: "make <friend> a healer" and "remove <friend>" produce a correct card. Friends added from a run keep a role inferred from their spec (D9).
  5. Two tabs: approving in one tab updates the card and the profile page in the other.
  6. Shared runner leakage: the greeting, class-guide and guild-summary text shouldn't mention profile changes. The profile tool is registered on the shared runner (D16).
  7. Bubble widths at your usual window sizes. Phone width still overflows because of the sidebar and header, which is sc-175.
  8. Design docs (sc-211, sc-212): read them and say if you want anything changed. I accepted them as the build gate (D2).
