User flow

Player asks a class-guide question→Knowledge search embeds the queries in time→Semantic and keyword hits merged→Answer from a smaller prompt
Connection drops mid-answer→Turn ends locally, note shown→Reconnect reloads the saved answer→Player asks again
Operator opens D6 and D8→Service graph shows real averages→Failed embeddings visible→No NaN gaps in ratio panels

Decisions and risks

  • All six steer tickets taken, plus "embed search queries concurrently" (sc-319), which the search ticket names as its likely cause. 10 points, under the usual 21 on purpose: the steer names the work and nothing unrelated is pulled in.
  • Four independent streams with disjoint files, run in parallel; the search fix and the D8 fix share files, so one developer does both.
  • Every story tier S: small, one area, no data model or security change. The prompt work runs on opus high because deciding what to cut without hurting answers is judgment, not mechanics.
  • First trial of the team knowledge layer: every brief carries the known facts for its files; developers add the facts they discover.
  • Risk: the search slowness may not be the sequential requests at all (one request alone hit 3 s live). Nils measures a live embedding before choosing the fix.
  • Risk: cutting the prompt can change answers. Every cut is listed with its reason; the untrusted-data rules stay verbatim; the live pass watches a kickoff and a guide question.
  • Drop first if time runs short: the smaller prompt (sc-367), then the reconnect fix (sc-376).
