Chat reliability

Branch sprint-2026-11-22 Base 4fc12a3 Product owner: Maren

Eight follow-ups the 11-20 QA sweep and live pass deferred. Goal: chat turns answer on both providers, never sit for minutes on a thinking-only call, and cost less per turn; the shared part of the cached prompt provably holds no workspace data.

Features

What the owner sees after this sprint, turn by turn:

Ask a question→Status line shows thinking, retries and fallbacks, even under spell cards→An empty or runaway model call is re-run once, within 3 minutes→Answer saved exactly as shown; reload matches→Next turn reads the earlier history from the cache

084 Model re-runs and the runner tier M Vera · opus high

Gemini empty answers (sc-586) first: a call that ends with nothing is re-run once before the "no answer" message. Then Claude output budget and time (sc-580): no single call runs past 180 s, every re-run counts toward the lookup-round cap, and the turn-stats line shows the re-runs. Then the runner side of the thinking-status edge cases (sc-582): no spliced status after a re-run, headings, and short answers that were wrongly treated as progress text.

  • An empty Gemini STOP is re-run once and counted; live: "is frost mage difficult?" in a fresh chat answers on Gemini.
  • A thinking-only Claude call is cut at the bound and re-run once at low effort; the re-run counts in the round cap.
  • budget_retries and empty_retries on the turn-stats line.

085 Status frames and turn costs tier M security review Edda · opus high

The socket and browser side of the thinking-status edge cases (sc-582): a cancel from a second tab, a lost progress frame, and retry/fallback labels that disappear once spell cards show. Plus two of the small chat-turn costs (sc-583): the per-turn settings read, and the status line moving out of App state so it stops re-rendering the whole chat.

  • The saved partial answer equals what a reload shows, after a second-tab cancel and after a lost frame.
  • Retry and fallback labels show in the status line under spell cards.
  • Thinking frames no longer re-render the App or the message list.

086 Mechanic notes and rankings tier S Ansel · opus medium

Boss-mechanic notes for Nymrissa Wavecaller, Kith'ix re-sourced, and a lookup that finds an ability named together with its boss (sc-585). A lint for the notes: allowed source hosts, no markup, no instruction-like text (sc-584, notes part). A live check whether whole-report rankings fail like the player list did, fixed only if they do (sc-587). Two token trims (sc-583): the damage-fields note and the mechanics tool description.

087 Claude history cache tier M security review Vera (fresh) · opus high

Each turn's first Claude call rewrites the whole cached history because the per-turn time block and the player-context preamble sit before it (sc-581). Measure the saving first, then move them after the history. A test pins that the shared cached head never holds the player-context preamble or workspace data (sc-584, cache part). Starts after 084 merges: same files.

Order and decisions

start 084, 085 and 086 run in parallel on disjoint files.
084 merged 087 starts on the merged tip (same files as 084).
all merged Full suite at the tip, QA sweep over the sprint diff, team-lead's live pass on Claude and Gemini; one fix round for both.
close-out Architecture docs, team facts, sprint review.
  • Split by file, not by ticket: the chat-turn code (model adapters, runner) has one owner at a time, so three tickets span two streams each. I track their states.
  • Gemini empty answers go first, as the owner asked; they share the re-run code with the Claude budget fix, so one developer does both.
  • No single Claude call may run longer than 180 s: a 5-11 minute wait is the failure the ticket describes.
  • Re-run counts go on the turn-stats log line, not into a new metric: the line is searchable in Loki, and a metric is scope the ticket doesn't need.
  • Two security reviews (status frames, history cache): one renders and stores model text, the other decides what sits in the cache shared across workspaces.
  • No scrum master: no file is shared in the same window.

Risks

  • 087 waits for 084: the long pole of the sprint.
  • The Gemini empty answer may not reproduce on demand; the re-run is proven with a fake, and the live check is best effort.
  • Moving the per-turn blocks after the history can grow every request by one preamble per turn; the brief caps that growth.
  • Nymrissa Wavecaller had no good source last sprint; the notes may stay thin.

Drop first if time runs short: the mechanics tool description trim (sc-583), then the breadth of the notes lint (sc-584).
