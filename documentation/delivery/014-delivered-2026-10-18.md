## What shipped

- **One shared cache.** Public Warcraft Logs data is cached once in Redis for every tenant. This replaces the
  in-process, tenant-scoped cache and supersedes feature 006 FR-019.
- **Seven key families** are reached only through `cache.get_or_load` / `get_many_or_load`:

  | Family | Fresh for | In-process memo |
  |---|---|---|
  | `char_id` | 24 h | 1 h |
  | `char_overview` | 10 min | — |
  | `char_reports` | 5 min | — |
  | `report_summary` | 24 h (2 min while live) | — |
  | `report_player` | 24 h (2 min while live) | — |
  | `oauth_token` | until 60 s before expiry | token memo |
  | `world_zones` | 1 h | 5 min |

- **Safe by default.**
  - Stampede lock: 10 concurrent misses make one request.
  - "Not found" answers are remembered for 60 s.
  - Corrupt entries are deleted and reloaded.
  - Fail-open: Redis down → straight to Warcraft Logs within 250 ms, with a 5 s circuit.
- **Admin stats:** `GET /api/admin/cache-stats?days=7` shows hit rate, load time and ok/review status per family,
  plus Redis memory and evictions.
- **"Check again"** bypasses the cache for a character, at most once a minute per user and character.
- **Warcraft Logs client:**
  - 429 and 5xx errors are retried.
  - It fails fast once the hourly budget is spent.
  - Every request logs its API cost.
  - Rate-limit checks reuse the budget that every response now carries.
- **Docker Compose:** a `redis:8-alpine` service (256 MB, LRU eviction, no persistence, health check) that the backend
  waits for. All settings are documented in `.env.example`.
