What worked

  • The product owner in planning. The owner asked for both viewpoints. Maren corrected two of my claims from the code: the .env ticket wasn't the P9 lever I thought it was, and its proposed fix wouldn't have worked. She also caught that the empty-answer bug was the other half of the prompt blow-up. The planning run cost 0.35 M, under 2% of the sprint, and launch needed no clarifying round.
  • Developers coordinating on the board without me. Nils found a pre-existing flaky test. Ansel fixed it in a commit on its own, and Nils cherry-picked that exact commit to get a clean receipt. Four board entries, no message to me.
  • Design answers up front. Both developers posted their design decisions (clarify: keys) before writing code. Maren overrode none of them.
  • Live results. The top-3 damage question went from 397k to 29k prompt tokens. The chart turn that used to end with no answer now answers with a chart (53k, was 220k). The recent-raid pre-fetch warmed 2 tables with no failures.
  • Owner preference 23 (ticket names) reached the product owner's file, the team-launch skill and Maren within minutes, and her reports use names.

What to change

  • Our own security fix caused the QA High. Moving the pre-fetch to the background lane (P7 rework) left the kickoff turn waiting behind it. The P7 review checks the diff, and the rework round had no review of its own. Change: a P7 rework that touches lanes, locks or ordering gets one targeted line in the end-sweep brief ("re-check the rework for liveness"). It's cheaper than a second review.
  • Timing-sensitive tests. Two flakes in one sprint came from wall-clock assumptions: an import-time NOW, and a 400 ms probe under a slow suite. Change: add "no wall-clock budgets under 1 s in tests, compute time per test" to developer.md.
  • GitNexus in a fresh worktree index returned nameless Python functions (Nils, skill:gitnexus-impact-analysis). I'll apply it as a local learning after the merge.
  • check.py's .env refusal can be relaxed once 047 is on main: the suite now switches .env off itself (Ansel, #54). I'll keep the refusal as a warning rather than drop it.
  • Live pass finding: the agent picked the wrong "last boss kill" in one of two turns on the same report. Filed as "Agent picks the wrong last boss kill" (sc-365). This is a model-behaviour bug, not a team one.
