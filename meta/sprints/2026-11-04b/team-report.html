What worked

Planning with the PO in the room. Maren read the code and caught the WebSocket gap in 415, the missing ruler, the unworkable cost rule and the seat-cost join PromQL can't do, before anyone built on them.
Contracts and owner keys before launch. Three streams touched Alloy, compose and Mimir rules, with no merge conflict and no blocked developer.
A mid-sprint owner request cost nothing extra. The two panels went to the developer already in that file, no ticket, merged with his story.
The telemetry earned its keep at once. The new knowledge-base dashboard showed every search as keyword-only at a 3.03 s p95, which is the embedding timeout (an old bug, sc-428).

What to change

Proofs on real-shaped data. Every stack script passed on fresh synthetic data; the real board directory (Oct 31 files) and real ratios broke at once. Seed old or odd data and assert values, not presence (Maren recorded this too).
Render checks taller than the page. My 2200 px screenshot left Grafana's off-screen panels blank and I reported a false failure. Nils diagnosed it in minutes.
Search before filing. My embedding-timeout ticket duplicated one from last sprint; Maren merged it.
Cheaper proof screenshots. One image per changed panel, not a full dashboard per fix (see the Cost tab).
