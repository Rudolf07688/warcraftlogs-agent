# P17 pilot: who repeats what (sprints 11-01 to 11-20)

Ledger: `ledger-pilot-2026-11-22.jsonl` (357 rows; three sonnet extractors, about 470k tokens
in all, from boards, QA triage, retros, team reports and the current memories). Each row has
a seat, a class, a severity and a ref. Quotes were spot-checked by the extractors. Attribution
is by stream owner and is uncertain in about 25 rows, which the extractors listed.

## 1. The first-draft rule fires on everything

"Same seat, same class, in 2 sprints" flags 38 seat×class pairs. Every developer who worked
often matches every broad class (unbounded, guard-bypass, logic-bug, error-handling). Two
reasons:
- **Volume follows workload.** Ansel, Tomas and Nils carried most of the security-core and
  telemetry streams from 11-01 to 11-05. More code meant more QA Lows.
- **Bug classes aren't habits.** "Unbounded" covers a regex, a metric label, a span processor
  and a rate limit. What can be taught is the habit behind them, not the bug type.

So 1:1s need a narrower test: medium or above; in at least 2 of the last 4 sprints; the same
**habit** (what the person did or skipped at the moment of work); not already falling after a
system fix; and a higher share than the rest of the team in that class.

## 3. One clear person case: Nils, bounds on new paths

- **13 medium+ "unbounded" findings in 7 of 10 sprints**, among them the only Critical in the
  ledger (11-05: the Pyroscope span processor grew without bound) and 2 Highs:
  - search with no pool or statement bound (044)
  - pre-fetch in the interactive lane with no cooldown (046)
  - neutralise over the whole raw table (050)
  - a quadratic redaction regex, an unbounded Host label, and a log record growing with the
    conversation (054)
  - span tags never cleaned up (059)
  - re-verify with no rate limit or in-flight cap, and a payload decompressed four times (072)
- **The habit:** a new path goes in without asking "what bounds this: time, size, rate, growth?"
- **Not covered today:** his memory holds only the narrow lesson ("regexes over untrusted text:
  bound every repeat"), and `developer.md` has no general bounds rule.
- **Still current:** his medium+ per sprint stayed about flat (4.3 → 4.0) while the others fell.
- **Also a system part:** Maren's briefs missed "no rate bound" as a hostile-input class
  (11-07b), and her memory notes D18 "keeps finding" it. The 1:1 goes with a brief item.
- **Smaller habit, same seat:** the failure or cancel path isn't covered (a timeout discards
  hits, a metric records only successes, the cancel path is missed in the fix too; 044, 059,
  059-fix). Raise it in the same 1:1, not as a second goal unless he picks it.

## 4. Not a coaching case: Maren's brief gaps

She had a brief-gap row in 8 of 10 sprints, but each one was a **different** gap: a missing file
grant (schemas.py, ws.py), hostile inputs, a spike sized as a build, a phrase two stories shared.
Each time she wrote the lesson into her memory or `product-owner.md`, and it didn't come back.
That is learning, not repeating. The ledger still shows the cost.

## 5. Other observations

- **Reworks without a review of their own:** twice a security rework introduced a new High
  (11-01b, the kickoff lane; 11-02, neutralise over the whole table). That's a system item: a
  short check of the rework diff.
- **Positives** (28 rows) cluster on Maren (fast grants, scoped decisions) and Ansel
  (live-check-pre scripts, root causes posted unasked). They're material for peer teaching.
